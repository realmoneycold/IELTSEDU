Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when my free trial ends?

You'll still have access to your dashboard and scores. You can use AI evaluations up to 3 times per day. To take new tests and get unlimited AI access, simply upgrade to a paid plan.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes! There are no long-term commitments. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, and you can cancel at any time.

How does the Humo/Uzcard payment work?

Transfer the payment amount to our card number shown on the payment page. Then submit your transfer details through the form. Our team verifies and activates your plan within 1-2 hours.

What's included in the AI evaluations?

Our AI evaluates your writing essays and speaking responses, giving you a band score estimate with detailed feedback on grammar, coherence, vocabulary, and task achievement — just like a real IELTS examiner.
